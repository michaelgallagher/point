# The 13 November batch: the iPhone after two days' use (branch `iphone-edits`)

Part of [the plan](../PLAN.md). Mike's list after using the iPhone app on the real vault, and what was done.

## Decided (13 November)

- **Your notes in reader mode** are set in the sans, in a softly tinted rounded box labelled "Notes", which takes the rule's place. The box's outside lines up with the article's text, and the notes sit 16 points in from it, so the box never asks more of the margins than the article does.
- **Mark read** sets `read:` to today and, for a clip still in the clips folder (Clippings), moves it to `Sources`. It's offered where reading ends (a button under the last line), in the reader's menu, and as a swipe on the note in lists. Nothing is added to the bars.
- **Near misses when clipping:** a page at the same address (by `source`) opens its note, as before. A page that may be in the vault at another address, or under a title all but the same, stops before anything's written and asks: open the note, clip anyway, or cancel. Both apps.
- **Lists of notes** say under each title what it is: a clip's format and folder ("Article · Sources"), another note's type ("Meeting"), else its folder. Clips have a check at the left once read, an empty circle before. The Media list has **Unread only** in its menu.

## Built

- **Hiding the keyboard** is a glass circle at the right of the format bar, under the thumb, not at the top right. The title has it too.
- **The notes sheet's Done** is blue.
- **The reader's bars** come back after 50 points of drag back, not 120, a little faster. The text no longer moves as they come back: while reading, the text view's insets are held as they are with the bars showing, rather than following the safe area as the bars come and go.
- **Search** offers, before typing, the notes last opened (less the one showing), then bookmarks, then recent searches.
- **The reader's menu:** Edit is no longer in the top bar; it's the menu's first item, on its own, while reading. **Properties** is in the menu for every note, not only clips, just above Move note (greyed while the Markdown is showing, when the properties are its text). Tapping a clip's byline opens the properties too. Closing the sheet saves at once, so highlighting works straight after.
- **Property chips** as on the Mac: a list or text property offers the values other notes give it (the most used as soon as the field's in use, then those starting with what's typed), and `[[` offers notes to link to. Tags and people as before, as chips of the same look.
- **Your notes' box** (`pointNotesBox`, EditorKit): the box drawn on the text column, and the notes indented inside it by `MarkdownStyler.notesInset` (quote bars, list markers and highlights moved in with them); the notes' fonts made sans, room above for the label and below the box, and the blank lines and marker between the box and the saved text squeezed to nothing. Quote bars and other blocks now leave out the room a paragraph asks above or below itself, so a quote at the start of your notes doesn't run into the label.
- **Mark read** (`Clipping.markingRead`, ClipKit): adds `read: <today>` at the end of the properties, or fills in an empty `read:` where the template left it, and leaves a read date alone; the rest of the file as it was. On the phone, from a list (`PhoneStore.markReadAndFile`) or the open note (`NoteSession.markReadAndFile`, which writes the file as it is on disk and shows it, as your notes do), then `PhoneStore.fileInSources`.
- **Near misses** (`ClipMatching`, ClipKit): an address's page (`pageKey`: host without `www.`, `m.` or `amp.`, path without a trailing slash, `/amp` or `index.html`, no query or fragment; YouTube by video ID), and titles compared without the site's name, accents, punctuation or small words (three quarters of the words shared, or all of the shorter one's four or more). Checked with the link's title before fetching and, if nothing was found then, with the page's own title after. The phone asks in an action sheet, the Mac in an alert.
- **Each note's type, format and read date** come from the index in one query (`SearchIndex.firstValues(of:)`), again whenever it changes.

## Tried

- `MarkingReadTests` (4), `ClipMatchingTests` (4) and a check of `firstValues` in `ListDefinitionTests`; every package's tests pass. Both apps build.
- In the Simulator, on a sample clip in Clippings: your notes in the box; Properties from the menu; the bars hidden and brought back by a 70-point drag with the text moving only with the finger; at the end, Mark read, move to Sources, after which the file had only `read: 2026-11-13` added and was in Sources; later, the box's edge level with the title and the article, Edit from the menu, and Properties from the menu of an ordinary note; Recent showing "Article · Sources" with a check, "Meeting", "Person"; Search's recent notes; `[[Sa` in a text property offering people and notes; a clip link to the same page at `http://www.…/` asking, and Open going to the note with nothing written.

## Second round (branch `iphone-edits-2`)

After the first round on the phone:

- The box's label is **Notes**, not "Your notes".
- The button where reading ends says **Mark read and move to Sources** (and the menu item with it).
- In lists, a clip's **check is at the right**, level with the middle of the row.
- **All / Unread** is a segmented control on the page, under the Media title, not in its menu; the title stays "Media" either way, and "Nothing unread" shows under the control.
- **Not reaching the end** of a note while reading: not reproduced in the Simulator with this build (a long clip and short ones, flung to the end, with the bars hidden, after Edit and Done). The likeliest cause was the build before this batch, whose insets moved as the bars came and went. As a guard, within two screens of the end the text view lays out whatever's left (`PhoneTextView.layOutEndIfNear`): restyling the whole note (an image arriving, the reader's type) leaves the layout below the screen a guess, and a guess that fell short would stop the scroll early. If it happens again, which note, and whether the bars were showing, will say more.

Then:

- **The reader's standard** is a step tighter: type 18 points (was 19), lines 1.3 (was 1.4), margins 18 points (was 24). Settings kept under the old standard (`ReaderSettings.standard`, now 2) take the new one for whatever was still as standard; anything chosen in Aa stays (tried with Sepia kept from old settings).
- **A page with one thing to do** shows it as its own button at the top right, not a ••• menu of one: sorting alone (Media and other types, Lists) is a ↑↓ button with the orders straight under it; New folder alone (Folders) is its button. Two or more, the ••• menu as before.

## Third round (branch `iphone-edits-3`)

- Search's first section before typing is **Recent notes**.
- **Completed and Bookmarked while reading:** a line under the byline, a green check and "Completed" for a clip with a `read` date, a filled bookmark and "Bookmarked" for a bookmarked note (`NoteHeader.showMarks`). Kept current as it's marked read, bookmarked, or the bookmarks change on another device.
- **The bookmark pill:** writing, a bookmarked note has a filled bookmark pill between its properties and what's related; tapped, it offers Remove bookmark.
- **The Mac:** a bookmarked note says "Bookmarked", with a filled bookmark in the icon column, between the properties' summary and the links line (or under the title, for a note with no properties); its menu removes the bookmark (`BookmarkReadout`). Builds; not looked at, as a second copy beside Mike's would share its settings.
- **Marked read, it opens at the top next time:** marking a clip read forgets its place (`ReadingPlaces.forget`), from the end of the article or a list's swipe, as Completed says it's been read. Going back into it before leaving (scrolling back up by hand) keeps the place as ever, and a read note's place is kept as any other's after that: only the act of marking read starts it over.
- Found, not changed: two of the clips copied from the vault have the marker with three dashes (`==--- Text ---==`), which the reader doesn't take for its marker, so it shows as highlighted text, and what's above isn't set as your notes.

## A slip that couldn't be undone (on `main`)

A swipe-typed word over a selection replaced the start of a post on the phone, and there was no way back: no undo button, and shaking offered nothing.

- **Undo and Redo** at the top right while writing (and while the note has anything to undo with the keyboard down), from the text view's own undo manager, kept current from its notifications. Tried: a typed slip undone with the keyboard hidden, on screen and in the file.
- **Earlier versions** in the note's menu (`NoteVersions`, VaultKit, outside the vault in the app's storage): the note as it was opened is kept as it first changes here, and the text being replaced on a restore. The latest 20 a note; they follow a rename or move. Each row says when, and the first line that differs from now; opened, the whole text and Restore. Restore is undoable. Tried: slipped, left, reopened, restored, undone.
- **Shake to undo** isn't answered yet: likely the history was gone (opening a note clears it), so iOS had nothing to offer. To check on the phone: type a word and shake straight away.
- Not built: earlier versions on the Mac.
