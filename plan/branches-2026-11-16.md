# Branches: what's active

Part of [the plan](../PLAN.md). Written 16 November 2026, 07:43 PST, from the local branches at that time. "Active" means not merged into `main`. `main` itself was at 83e9ad1.

## The main line

- **`main`**: the shipping app, with the Liquid Glass header. It includes the narrow-window tab work from `glass-narrow-tabs`.

## Flat-header experiments

None of these is merged into `main`. Each is built on the one listed below it. The whole story is in `plan/exploration-flat-header.md`, which is on these branches but not on `main`.

- **`header-scroll-tabs`** (16 November, pushed): the newest flat header. Built on `header-narrow-tabs`, with these changes:
  - the tab row scrolls sideways instead of rolling extra tabs into a "›› N" menu;
  - the sidebar button and back and forward hide below 580 points, with or without a split;
  - the "…" menu floats at the note's top right instead of sitting in the bar;
  - 4 points more room left of the sidebar button.
- **`header-two-rows`** (15 November, not pushed): two rows.
  - The top row always shows the tabs, even one, with a split button at the window's far right.
  - A thinner second row holds back and forward, the folder path, a bookmark button and "…".
  - The tabs have no grey rail.
  - **The branch has nothing of its own.** The work was never committed; it lives only in `build/patches/header-two-rows.patch`, which isn't backed up. To bring it back: `git switch header-two-rows && git apply build/patches/header-two-rows.patch`.
- **`header-narrow-tabs`** (15 November, pushed): the single-row flat header, tuned for 420–500-point windows.
  - Tabs shrink in stages and roll extras into "›› N".
  - Titles are shortened by what tells them apart.
  - The sidebar button and back and forward hide in narrow windows.
- **`header-compact-tabs`** (14 November, pushed): tabs back in the top row, with tighter buttons and tabs, and Split back in the "…" menu.
- **`header-tabs-below`** (14 November, pushed): tabs in their own row below the bar, a split button beside "…", and the path back above the title.
- **`header-redesign`** (13 November, pushed): the first flat header: plain icon buttons instead of glass, tabs shaped like sidebar rows, the compact title bar, and the path in the header.

## Merged, but still around

- **`glass-narrow-tabs`**: the narrow-tab work on the glass header ([the brief](narrow-tabs-for-glass.md)). It's wholly in `main`, and still checked out in `build/worktrees/glass-narrow-tabs`.

## Older experiments, never merged

- **`animating-the-cursor`** (3–4 November): a custom, animated text cursor; 8 commits refining its motion and blink, and fixing glitches.
- **`skinny-bar`** (6 November): a test of a thinner top bar.
- **`simple-but-bigger`** (24–25 October): a more compact layout, then reworked header sizing.
- **`tighter-and-lighter`** (24 October): the compact layout and small fixes. Its three commits are the first three of `simple-but-bigger`, so that branch covers it.
- **`capsules`** (24 October): a test of rounded capsule shapes everywhere.

## Everything else

About 40 more local branches are wholly merged into `main`: the iPhone work, images, lists, types, the 8 November stages, the clipper, reader mode and others. They could be deleted to tidy the list.
